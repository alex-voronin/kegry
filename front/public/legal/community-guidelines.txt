Community Guidelines

Wibblycorn is for genuine friendships and social connections - keep it respectful, safe, and fun for everyone. 
1. Core Rules:
1.1. Be authentic - No fake profiles, impersonation, or misleading information.
1.2. Be respectful - No harassment, bullying, hate speech, discrimination, threats, or any abusive behavior (online or offline).
1.3. No explicit or sexual content -  Profiles, messages, photos, and event descriptions must be appropriate for a general friendship audience.
1.4. No scams, spam, or commercial activity - No selling, advertising, solicitation, or fraudulent schemes.
1.5. Respect privacy and boundaries - Don’t share others’ personal info without consent; respect when someone says no or wants space.

2. Safety First - Especially for In-Person Meetups
2.1. All activities are user-initiated and voluntary - we don’t organise or supervise them.
2.2. Prioritize your own safety: meet in public, tell a friend your plans, use your own transport.
2.3. Do not pressure anyone to drink alcohol or consume substances.
2.4. Respect personal boundaries at all times.
2.5. If anything feels unsafe or uncomfortable - leave immediately and report it.
2.6. Follow local laws, venue rules, and legal drinking age requirements.

3. Reporting & Enforcement
3.1. Report any violations or unsafe behavior directly in the app.
3.2. We review reports seriously and act accordingly: content removal, warnings, temporary restrictions, account suspension, or permanent ban.
3.3. Repeated or severe violations (e.g., threats, harassment, illegal content) may lead to reporting to law enforcement.

Violations of these Guidelines may result in account actions up to permanent ban. We reserve the right to update these Guidelines, significant changes will be notified in-app.
